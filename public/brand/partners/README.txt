Partner logos shown along the bottom of the "I'm attending" flyer (/flyer).

Drop the image files here (PNG with a transparent or white background works
best, at least 300 px tall). They appear left to right in file-name order, so
number them, for example:

  1-space-in-africa.png
  2-afsa.png
  3-ministry-higher-education-senegal.png

No code change is needed: the flyer picks up whatever images are in this
folder after the next deploy. With no images here, the flyer keeps its
original layout.
